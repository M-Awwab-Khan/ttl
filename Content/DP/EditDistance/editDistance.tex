\textbf{Edit Distance}
Given two strings $s_1$ and $s_2$, find the minimum number of operations (insert, delete, replace) required to transform $s_1$ into $s_2$.

\textbf{Time Complexity: $O(mn)$}
\textbf{Space Complexity: $O(mn)$}
